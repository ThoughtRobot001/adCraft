## What These Benchmarks Tell Us

**The good news**: AdCraft's architecture (narrative arc, spring physics, dark glassmorphic SaaS aesthetic, kinetic text, metric counters) is **on the right track**. Video 6 literally validates our pipeline structure.

**The honest gap**: These benchmarks are heavily reliant on:
1. **Cursor interaction choreography** — the simulated mouse clicking through UI is what makes them feel "real" and not just animated slides
2. **Device mockups** — 3D phones and tilted device frames with real screenshots composited inside
3. **Light-mode support** — half these videos use white/bright backgrounds, not just dark
4. **VFX transitions** — particle systems, warp effects, and smooth cross-blends between scenes

These are the capabilities that would close the gap from "good programmatic motion" to "professional motion designer quality."
